Note that cricket chirp calculations are based on extrapolation of enzyme activity rather than Dolbear's law. Also note that crickets are likely to be physically incapable of chirping at various temperatures. This converter just aims to give you the theoretical number for fun, rather than a practical value for a practical purpose..
